**Rabbithollow Code Studio™** and **Ethical World™** are claimed product/brand identifiers used by the project owner.

The ™ symbol is used as a branding claim. It does **not** state that either mark is registered in every jurisdiction.

## Permitted reference

You may use the names in factual statements such as:

- “I use Ethical World.”
- “This file was exported from Ethical World.”
- “This project interoperates with Ethical World.”

## Not permitted without written permission

You may not use the names, logos, SVG mark, product appearance, or confusingly similar branding to:

- present a fork or derivative as an official Ethical World release;
- rebrand the software as your own product;
- imply endorsement, partnership, or ownership;
- remove the Rabbithollow Code Studio™ attribution from redistributed material where attribution is required;
- market a modified or competing derivative under Ethical World / Rabbithollow branding.

The software license and this brand policy are separate: permission to use the software does not grant ownership of the brand.
